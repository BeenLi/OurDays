三步,开始你们的日历

Three steps to a shared calendar

1

先设好自己

起一个昵称,选一张照片或两个字做头像。这时还不需要知道对方是谁。

Pick a nickname and an avatar. You don't need to know who your partner is yet.

2

发一条链接配对

一条 iCloud 共享链接,对方点开即配对;TA 的昵称和头像自动同步过来。

Send one iCloud share link; your partner taps it and you're paired. Their name and avatar sync over.

3

挑出要共享的日历

勾选要让对方看到的那几本系统日历,其余私人日程绝不外露。

Tick the system calendars your partner may see; everything else stays private.

开源、免费,没有服务器

OurDays 完全开源,没有自建服务器,也没有账号体系:数据只在你和伴侣之间通过 Apple iCloud 私密同步。不含广告 SDK、第三方分析或跨应用追踪。它唯一的运行成本是每年的 Apple 开发者年费,所以可以一直免费、一直开着。

OurDays is fully open source, runs no server and has no accounts of its own: data syncs only through your own iCloud. No ad SDKs, analytics, or tracking. Its only running cost is the yearly Apple developer fee — which is why it can stay free, and stay up.
