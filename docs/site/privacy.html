OurDays 隐私政策

最后更新:2026 年 6 月 22 日 · Last updated: June 22, 2026

OurDays 是一款私密的双人日历应用。它帮助两位受邀用户并排查看各自选择的日历、共享活动邀请,并对共享活动进行评论。

OurDays is a private couple calendar app. The app helps two invited users view selected calendars side by side, share event invitations, and comment on shared events.

应用使用的数据

OurDays 仅在你授予日历权限后访问你明确选择的日历。根据你的隐私设置,共享的日历镜像可能包含活动标题、时间、地点、备注、网址、来源日历名称和日历颜色。

OurDays 还可能存储应用内创建的邀请、评论、已读状态,以及基础的成员资料信息,例如显示名称、头像颜色,以及 CloudKit 共享所需的 iCloud 账户标识符。

OurDays may access the calendars you explicitly select after granting calendar permission. Depending on your privacy setting, shared calendar mirrors may include event title, time, location, notes, URL, source calendar name, and calendar color. OurDays may also store app-created invitations, comments, read state, and basic member profile information such as display name, avatar color, and an iCloud account identifier needed for CloudKit sharing.

数据如何存储

OurDays 将数据存储在你的设备本地,以及通过 CloudKit 存储在 Apple iCloud 中,以便所选信息在你与受邀伴侣之间同步。共享数据仅对包含在该 CloudKit 共享中的用户可见。OurDays 不运营独立的服务器,也不出售个人数据。

OurDays stores data locally on your device and in Apple iCloud using CloudKit so that selected information can sync between you and your invited partner. Shared data is visible only to the users included in the CloudKit share. OurDays does not operate an independent server and does not sell personal data.

跟踪与广告

OurDays 不包含广告 SDK、第三方分析 SDK,也不进行跨应用跟踪。

OurDays does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

数据共享

共享的日历镜像、邀请和评论仅与你通过 iCloud 共享邀请的伴侣共享。数据也可能由 Apple 作为 iCloud、App Store 和设备操作系统服务的一部分进行处理。

Shared calendar mirrors, invitations, and comments are shared only with the partner you invite through iCloud sharing. Data may also be processed by Apple as part of iCloud, App Store, and device operating system services.

删除数据

你可以通过从设备删除 OurDays 来停止使用。你也可以通过移除 iCloud 共享来停止共享。你在本地"日历"中创建的活动仍由你在 Apple 日历中掌控。

You can stop using OurDays by deleting the app from your device. You can also stop sharing by removing the iCloud share. Calendar events created in your local Calendar remain under your control in Apple Calendar.
