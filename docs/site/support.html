OurDays 支持

最后更新:2026 年 6 月 22 日 · Last updated: June 22, 2026

OurDays 是一款面向受邀情侣的私密共享日历:两人将所选的 Apple 日历活动镜像到一个私密的 iCloud 共享中,并排查看彼此的日程,发送活动邀请,并对共享活动进行评论。

OurDays is a private shared calendar for invited couples: two people mirror selected Apple Calendar events into a private iCloud share, view both schedules side by side, send event invitations, and comment on shared events.

获取帮助

通过 App Store 页面中标注的支持邮箱联系我们;如果你使用的是 TestFlight 版本,可打开 TestFlight,选择 OurDays,使用"发送 Beta 版反馈"。

报告问题时,请包含:

  • 你想做什么。
  • 实际发生了什么。
  • 你的 iOS 版本。
  • 是否已启用日历访问与 iCloud。
  • 有助于说明问题的截图。

Report an issue by email, or — if you are using a TestFlight build — open TestFlight, select OurDays, and use "Send Beta Feedback". Please include what you were trying to do, what happened instead, your iOS version, whether calendar access and iCloud are enabled, and screenshots if they help.

常见要求

OurDays 需要:

  • iOS 18 或更高版本。
  • 设备上已登录 iCloud 账户。
  • 日历访问权限。
  • 已启用 iCloud 云盘以支持 CloudKit 同步。

OurDays requires iOS 18 or later, an iCloud account signed in on the device, calendar access permission, and iCloud Drive enabled for CloudKit syncing.

隐私 · Privacy

关于日历数据与 iCloud 共享的详情,请阅读 OurDays 隐私政策。
